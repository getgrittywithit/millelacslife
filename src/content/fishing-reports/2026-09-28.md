## Overview

Fall fishing on Mille Lacs is heating up as the water cools down. The walleye bite has been excellent this week, with fish moving into more predictable patterns as water temperatures drop into the upper 50s.

## What's Working

The classic fall pattern is in full effect. Walleye are staging on rocky points and mud flats in 12-18 feet of water. Jigs tipped with minnows have been the top producer, though Lindy rigs with leeches are also effective.

## Looking Ahead

Expect the bite to continue improving through early October. As water temps drop into the low 50s, walleye will become even more aggressive before the pre-ice slowdown.

## Regulations Reminder

Check current MN DNR regulations before heading out. Mille Lacs has specific slot limits that change seasonally. [View current regulations](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/fishing/mille_lacs.html)
