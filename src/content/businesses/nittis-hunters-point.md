Nitti's Hunters Point Resort is a legendary Mille Lacs Lake destination known for exceptional walleye fishing. The resort features modern cabins and an on-site restaurant serving bar food and pizza.

## Why Anglers Choose Nitti's

- Prime location on the south shore's best walleye spots
- Professional fishing guides who know the lake
- Full-service fish cleaning station
- Comfortable, well-maintained cabins

The on-site restaurant is a popular spot for anglers to swap fishing stories over a cold beer and fresh pizza.
