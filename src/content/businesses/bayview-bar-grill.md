BayView Bar and Grill has been an icon on the south end of Mille Lacs Lake for more than a century, established in 1897.

## A Lake Tradition

This year-round destination attracts tourists, lake homeowners, cabin owners, fishermen, and hunters alike. The stunning location offers beautiful views of Mille Lacs Lake.

## Amenities

- **Boat Dock**: Pull up right to the restaurant
- **Full Bar**: Cold drinks and cocktails
- **Liquor Store**: Stock up for your cabin
- **Outdoor Seating**: Enjoy the lake breeze

Whether you're coming off the lake after a day of fishing or looking for a casual dinner with a view, BayView delivers.
