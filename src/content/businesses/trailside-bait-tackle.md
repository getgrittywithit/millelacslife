Trailside Convenience is a classic Mom & Pop shop located next to the Soo Line trail on Highway 47.

## One-Stop Shop

- **Bait & Tackle**: Full selection for Mille Lacs fishing
- **Rental Gear**: Don't have gear? No problem
- **Fuel**: Gas, diesel, and propane
- **Food**: Hot and cold food available
- **Tire Shop**: Emergency tire service

A convenient stop for everything you need before hitting the lake or the trail.
