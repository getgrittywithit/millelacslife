Beachside Resort offers 1, 2, and 3 bedroom cabin rentals on the scenic southern sandy shores of Mille Lacs Lake.

## Rentals Available

- **Cabin Rentals**: 1, 2, and 3 bedroom options
- **Pontoon Rentals**: Explore the lake at your own pace
- **RV Park**: Full hookup sites available

The south shore is known for its sandy beaches and beautiful sunset views over the lake.
