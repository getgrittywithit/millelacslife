Garrison Sports Bait and Tackle is located right across the road from Mille Lacs Lake in the heart of Garrison, Minnesota.

## Launch Fishing

In summer, Garrison Sports offers guided fishing trips on their 36-foot steel hull launch:
- 3 trips daily
- Walleye, perch, northern pike, and muskie
- Rods, reels, and bait included
- Perfect for groups and families

## Bait & Tackle

Full selection of live bait and tackle for Mille Lacs fishing.
