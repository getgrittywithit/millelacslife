Mugg's of Mille Lacs is a beloved main street bar in Wahkon, featuring local history and great bar food.

## Known For

- **Fresh Hand-Pattied Burgers**: Made to order, not frozen
- **Chicken Sandwiches**: Fresh and crispy
- **Steak Sandwiches**: A local favorite

## Local Vibe

The walls tell the history of Wahkon, making this more than just a bar - it's a piece of Mille Lacs heritage. A favorite pitstop for anglers and locals alike.
