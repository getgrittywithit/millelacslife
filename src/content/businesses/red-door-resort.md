The Red Door Resort is a family-oriented vacation and fishing resort located on Mille Lacs Lake in central Minnesota, just two hours from Minneapolis-St. Paul.

## Accommodations

- **The Stone House**: 3-bedroom cabin starting at $274.95/night (2-night minimum)
- **2-Bedroom Cabins**: Starting at $174.95/night (2-night minimum)

All cabins feature clean, modern, comfortable accommodations with beautiful lake views.

## Year-Round Activities

- World-class walleye fishing
- Ice fishing packages in winter
- Water sports in summer
- Access to hunting, snowmobile, and ATV trails
