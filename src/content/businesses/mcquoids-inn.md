McQuoid's Inn is located at the southeast corner of Mille Lacs Lake in Isle, Minnesota. The resort offers a variety of accommodations including 5-bedroom executive and standard log condos, 2-bedroom cabins, and comfortable hotel rooms.

## Services

- **Launch Fishing**: Professional launch service for guided fishing trips
- **Fishing Guides**: Expert local guides who know the lake
- **Event Center**: Perfect for weddings, reunions, and corporate events

McQuoid's has been a Mille Lacs institution for decades, consistently ranked among the top fishing resorts on the lake.
