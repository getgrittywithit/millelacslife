Tutt's Bait & Tackle has proudly served the Mille Lacs Lake area for over 100 years, making it one of Minnesota's most legendary bait shops.

## What We Offer

- **Live Bait**: Full selection of minnows, leeches, and crawlers
- **Tackle**: Rods, reels, line, and tackle chosen specifically for Mille Lacs
- **Hunting Supplies**: Seasonal hunting gear and licenses
- **Rentals**: Boat and ice castle rentals available

## Local Knowledge

The staff at Tutt's knows Mille Lacs inside and out. Stop in for the latest fishing reports and find out what's working on the lake.
